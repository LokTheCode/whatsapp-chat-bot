Q: How do I punch in?
A: Open the app > Attendance > tap Punch In. Before punching, make sure Location is turned ON and Camera permission is allowed. Take a clear selfie if your company requires it, then confirm. You will see a success message with the time.

Q: How do I punch out?
A: Attendance > tap Punch Out at the end of your shift. Location and camera must be enabled, the same as for punch in. Your working hours for the day are shown after you punch out.

Q: Why does the app ask me to enable location before punching?
A: Your company uses location-based attendance to confirm you are at the office or an approved site. Turn on GPS/Location in your phone settings, set it to High Accuracy and allow the app to access location "While using the app".

Q: How do I enable location permission on my phone?
A: Android: Settings > Apps > HRM App > Permissions > Location > Allow while using the app, and turn on Location in quick settings. iPhone: Settings > Privacy > Location Services > ON, then HRM App > While Using the App.

Q: How do I enable camera permission for punching?
A: Android: Settings > Apps > HRM App > Permissions > Camera > Allow. iPhone: Settings > HRM App > Camera > ON. Then reopen the app and try punching again.

Q: Why is a selfie required while punching?
A: A selfie prevents buddy punching. Your company can set it as mandatory, optional or off. If it is mandatory, you cannot punch without a clear photo of your face.

Q: What does "Location permission denied" mean?
A: The app cannot read your location. Allow location permission in phone settings and turn GPS on. If you chose "Don't allow" earlier, open app permissions and change it to "Allow while using the app".

Q: What does "You are outside the allowed area" mean?
A: Your company set a geofence (an allowed radius around the office). Move closer to the office or approved site and punch again. If you are in the right place, wait 30 seconds for GPS to settle. Otherwise raise a regularization request.

Q: My GPS accuracy is low or my location is wrong. What should I do?
A: Turn on High Accuracy mode, switch Wi-Fi on (it improves location), move to an open area, wait up to 30 seconds and retry. Avoid using VPN or battery saver while punching.

Q: Can I punch from the web or laptop?
A: Only if your admin has enabled Web Punch. Log in on the web portal > Attendance > Punch In. The browser will ask for location and camera permission, so choose Allow.

Q: I forgot to punch in or out. How do I fix it?
A: Attendance > select the date > Regularize. Enter the correct in/out time and a reason, then submit. Your manager approves it. Check your company's limit on regularizations per month.

Q: What is a late mark and when is it applied?
A: A late mark is recorded when you punch in after shift start time plus the grace period. A set number of late marks in a month can convert to a half-day deduction, depending on your company policy.

Q: What is the grace time?
A: Grace time is the extra minutes after shift start during which punching in is not counted as late. Your company sets this value.

Q: How are half-day and absent decided?
A: Working hours below the half-day threshold marks Absent, between half-day and full-day threshold marks Half Day, and above the full-day threshold marks Present. Thresholds are defined in your attendance policy.

Q: How is overtime calculated?
A: Hours worked beyond the shift end are counted as overtime if your company has enabled overtime and your manager approves it. The rate comes from your salary structure.

Q: How do I record a break?
A: If breaks are enabled, tap Start Break and End Break on the Attendance screen. Break time is deducted from working hours when your policy says so.

Q: How do I mark work from home?
A: Attendance > Punch In > select Work From Home if enabled, or apply a WFH request from Requests > Work From Home. Manager approval may be needed.

Q: Can I punch when I have no internet?
A: If Offline Punch is enabled, your punch is saved on the phone with the time and location and syncs when the internet returns. Keep the app installed and do not clear data until it syncs.

Q: Face recognition is not working. What should I do?
A: Clean the camera, face the light, remove mask, cap or sunglasses and hold the phone at eye level. If it still fails, punch with a normal selfie if allowed or ask HR to reset your face data.

Q: How do I change the device I punch from?
A: Each employee is linked to one device if Device Lock is on. Ask HR to unbind your old device from Employee > Device Management, then log in on the new phone.

Q: Biometric machine punches are not showing in the app. Why?
A: Biometric data syncs every few minutes. Check that the machine is online. HR can tap Sync Now in Settings > Devices. If the machine was offline, data appears once it reconnects.

Q: Where can I see my attendance history?
A: Attendance > Calendar or History. Pick the month to see in/out times, working hours, late marks and leave days.

Q: What do the colors on the attendance calendar mean?
A: Green is Present, Red is Absent, Orange is Half Day, Blue is Leave, Grey is Weekly Off or Holiday, and Yellow means Pending regularization. Colors can be slightly different in your company.

Q: Why do I see a duplicate punch?
A: Two punches in a short time can happen if the network was slow. The system keeps the first valid punch. Report it to HR if your hours are wrong.

Q: What is auto punch-out?
A: If you forget to punch out, the system can automatically close the day at shift end or midnight and mark it for correction. Raise a regularization to set the correct out time.

Q: How does field staff or live tracking work?
A: For field employees the app records location when you punch and, if enabled, at intervals during duty. Keep Location on and set battery optimization to Unrestricted for the app.

Q: The app shows "Fake GPS detected". What do I do?
A: Turn off any mock location, GPS spoofing or developer location settings and restart the phone. Then punch again. Repeated detections are reported to HR.

Q: The time on the punch looks wrong. Why?
A: The app uses server time, but a wrong phone date or time can block punching. Turn on "Automatic date and time" and the correct time zone in phone settings.

Q: How can a manager see team attendance?
A: Menu > Team > Attendance. Pick a date to view who is present, absent, late or on leave, and open a person to view their punch photo and location.

Q: How do I review a team member's punch photo or location?
A: Team > Attendance > select the employee and date > tap the punch entry. You can view the selfie, map location and time.
