Q: How do I view or download my payslip?
A: Menu > Payroll > Payslips. Select the month and tap Download to save the PDF. Payslips appear after payroll is published by HR.

Q: When will I get my salary?
A: Salary is credited on the payroll date set by your company, usually the last working day or first week of next month. You get a notification when payroll is processed.

Q: How do I see my salary structure?
A: Payroll > Salary Structure shows basic, HRA, allowances and deductions for your CTC.

Q: What deductions are shown on my payslip?
A: Typical deductions are PF, ESI, Professional Tax, TDS and Loss of Pay. Open any payslip to see each amount.

Q: How is PF (Provident Fund) calculated?
A: PF is normally 12 percent of basic salary (capped as per your company setting) for both employee and employer contribution. See the PF line on your payslip.

Q: How is ESI calculated?
A: ESI applies if your gross salary is within the ESI limit. Employee share is 0.75 percent and employer share is 3.25 percent of gross wages.

Q: How is TDS decided?
A: TDS is based on your yearly taxable income, tax regime and the investment declarations you submit. Update declarations in Payroll > Tax Declaration to reduce excess deduction.

Q: How do I submit my tax declaration?
A: Payroll > Tax Declaration > choose the financial year, enter investments like 80C, HRA rent and others, upload proofs and Submit before the last date set by HR.

Q: How do I download Form 16?
A: Payroll > Tax Documents > Form 16. It is available after the financial year closes and HR uploads it.

Q: How do I find my bonus or incentive details?
A: Bonus and incentives show in the payslip under Earnings for the month they are paid. For details ask HR via Support > Raise Ticket.

Q: What is arrears and why is it in my payslip?
A: Arrears are previous unpaid amounts, usually from a salary revision applied with a back date, paid along with the current month.

Q: Why is my salary on hold?
A: Salary can be put on hold for pending exit clearance, missing documents or attendance approvals. Contact HR to know the reason and release date.

Q: How do I request a salary advance or loan?
A: Payroll > Advance/Loan > Request. Enter the amount and instalments. After approval the instalments are deducted from your monthly salary.

Q: My salary amount looks wrong. What should I do?
A: Check your attendance, leave and LOP days for the month, then compare deductions on the payslip. If it still looks wrong, raise a ticket in Support with the month and payslip screenshot.

Q: How do I update my bank account for salary?
A: Profile > Bank Details > Edit, enter account number, IFSC and upload a cancelled cheque. HR verifies it. The new account is used from the next payroll run.

Q: How does HR run payroll?
A: Admin or HR: Payroll > Run Payroll > select the month > Review attendance and inputs > Process > Publish Payslips. Lock attendance before running.

Q: What is the CTC breakup?
A: CTC is the total yearly cost to the company: gross salary plus employer PF, ESI and other benefits. Payroll > Salary Structure shows the monthly and yearly split.

Q: How is Loss of Pay deducted from salary?
A: LOP amount is monthly gross divided by the days in the payroll month (or fixed days per your policy) multiplied by LOP days.
