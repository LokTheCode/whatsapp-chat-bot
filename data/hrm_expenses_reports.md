Q: How do I submit an expense claim?
A: Menu > Expenses > New Claim. Choose the category, amount and date, attach the bill photo and Submit. Your manager and finance approve it.

Q: What expense categories are available?
A: Common categories are Travel, Food, Fuel, Accommodation, Client Meeting and Miscellaneous. Your company may add more.

Q: How do I upload receipts for an expense?
A: In the claim form tap Attach, take a photo or choose a file (JPG, PNG or PDF up to 5 MB). Keep the bill readable.

Q: How do I check the status of my expense claim?
A: Expenses > My Claims shows each claim as Pending, Approved, Rejected or Paid.

Q: My expense claim was rejected. What can I do?
A: Open the claim to read the rejection reason. Correct the details or add the missing bill and resubmit as a new claim.

Q: When will the approved expense be paid?
A: Approved expenses are paid with your next payroll or through a separate reimbursement run, as set by your company.

Q: How do I claim mileage for travel?
A: Expenses > New Claim > Mileage. Enter start and end location or distance and the vehicle type. The amount is calculated using the company rate per km.

Q: How do I request a travel advance?
A: Expenses > Advance > Request. Enter the amount and purpose. Settle it later by attaching your expense bills.

Q: How do I download the attendance report?
A: Admin or manager: Reports > Attendance > choose the date range, branch or team > Download as Excel or PDF.

Q: How do I get the leave report?
A: Reports > Leave > select the date range and leave type, then download. It shows leave taken and balances for each employee.

Q: How do I get the payroll report?
A: Reports > Payroll > select the month > Download. Available reports include salary register, bank advice and PF/ESI statements.

Q: Where can I see the headcount report?
A: Reports > Headcount shows total employees by department, branch and status, including joiners and leavers.

Q: How can I export a report?
A: Open any report and tap Export in the top right. Choose Excel, CSV or PDF. Large reports are emailed to you.

Q: Can I schedule a report to be emailed automatically?
A: Reports > open a report > Schedule. Select daily, weekly or monthly, add recipients and Save.
