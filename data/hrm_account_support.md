Q: How do I log in to the app?
A: Open the app, enter your registered mobile number or email, then the password or OTP. If you belong to more than one company, choose the company after login.

Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: On the login screen tap Forgot Password, enter your mobile or email, enter the OTP sent to you and set a new password.

Q: How do I change my password?
A: Profile > Settings > Change Password. Enter the old password and the new one twice, then Save.

Q: I am not receiving the OTP. What should I do?
A: Check mobile network and spam folder if it is by email, wait 60 seconds and tap Resend. Make sure your number is correct. If it still does not come, ask HR to verify your registered number.

Q: How do I update my mobile number or email?
A: Profile > Edit > Mobile or Email, enter the new value and verify it with an OTP. Some companies need HR approval.

Q: How do I change my profile photo?
A: Profile > tap your photo > choose Camera or Gallery > Save. Companies using face attendance may need HR to approve the new photo.

Q: How do I change the app language?
A: Profile > Settings > Language and pick your language. The app restarts to apply it.

Q: How do I log out from all devices?
A: Profile > Settings > Security > Logout from all devices. You will need to log in again everywhere.

Q: How do I control notifications?
A: Profile > Settings > Notifications. Turn on or off push notifications for attendance, leave, payroll and announcements. Also allow notifications in phone settings.

Q: How do I switch between my companies?
A: Tap your company name at the top of the Home screen or Profile > Switch Company, and choose another company. You can also type "change company" here.

Q: How do I enable two-factor authentication?
A: Profile > Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication > Enable. Verify the OTP sent to your mobile.

Q: The app is crashing or not opening. What should I do?
A: Update the app from Play Store or App Store, restart the phone, clear the app cache and check that you have free storage. If the problem remains, reinstall and log in again.

Q: How do I update the app?
A: Open Play Store or App Store, search for the HRM app and tap Update. Turn on auto-update to stay on the latest version.

Q: How do I contact HR?
A: Menu > Support > Contact HR shows your HR's phone and email. You can also raise a ticket for a written record.

Q: How do I raise a support ticket?
A: Menu > Support > Raise Ticket. Choose a category, describe the problem, attach a screenshot and Submit. You can track the status under My Tickets.

Q: How is my personal data protected?
A: Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest and visible only to authorised HR and managers in your company. Read the full policy under Profile > Settings > Privacy Policy.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Account deletion must be requested through HR because it affects payroll and compliance records. Raise a ticket under Support > Raise Ticket > Account.

Q: Where can I view my profile and job details?
A: Tap Profile at the bottom of the app to see your personal info, job details, bank details and documents.

Q: How do I view company announcements and policies?
A: Menu > Announcements shows notices from HR. Policies are under Menu > Documents > Company Policies.

Q: How do I see my reporting manager and team?
A: Profile > Job Details shows your reporting manager. Menu > Team shows your team if you are a manager.

Q: How do I download my employment documents, like an offer or experience letter?
A: Profile > Documents lists letters uploaded by HR. If a letter is missing, raise a ticket under Support > Documents.
