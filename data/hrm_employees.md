Q: How do I add a new employee?
A: Admin or HR only. 1) Open Menu > Employees > Add Employee. 2) Fill name, mobile, email, joining date, department, designation and reporting manager. 3) Choose shift, leave policy and salary structure. 4) Tap Save. The employee gets an SMS/email invite to set a password.

Q: How do I add many employees at once (bulk import)?
A: Go to Employees > Import. Download the Excel template, fill one row per employee, upload it and tap Validate. Fix any rows marked red, then tap Import. Maximum 500 rows per file.

Q: How do I edit an employee's details?
A: Employees > select the employee > Edit. Change the fields and tap Save. Changes to salary or designation can be given an effective date.

Q: How do I deactivate an employee?
A: Employees > select employee > More > Deactivate. Choose the last working day and reason. Attendance and payroll stop after that date, but history is kept.

Q: How do I reactivate a former employee?
A: Employees > filter Inactive > select the person > Reactivate. Set the new joining date. Old records remain linked to the same employee ID.

Q: Can I delete an employee permanently?
A: Employees with attendance or payroll history cannot be deleted; deactivate them instead. Only employees added by mistake with no records can be deleted from More > Delete.

Q: How is the employee ID generated?
A: The ID is auto-generated using the prefix and number series set by admin in Settings > Employee ID Format. You can enter a custom ID while adding an employee if manual IDs are enabled.

Q: How do I assign an employee to a department or branch?
A: Open the employee profile > Job Details > Department / Branch, choose from the list and Save. Admin can create new departments in Settings > Organization.

Q: How do I assign or change a reporting manager?
A: Employee profile > Job Details > Reporting Manager. Pick the manager and Save. Leave, attendance and expense approvals will go to the new manager from that moment.

Q: How do I upload employee documents like Aadhaar, PAN or offer letter?
A: Employee profile > Documents > Upload. Choose the document type, attach a PDF or photo (max 5 MB) and Save. Employees can also upload their own documents from Profile > Documents.

Q: What is the onboarding checklist?
A: After adding an employee, open Onboarding to see tasks such as document collection, bank details, asset issue and policy acceptance. Mark each task complete; the employee can view their pending tasks in the app.

Q: How do I transfer an employee to another branch or company?
A: Employee profile > More > Transfer. Select the new branch, effective date and reporting manager. Attendance and leave balance move with the employee unless your admin has set a reset.

Q: How do I set or extend an employee's probation?
A: Employee profile > Job Details > Probation. Set the end date, or tap Extend to add more days. HR gets a reminder 7 days before probation ends.

Q: How do I process an employee's resignation or exit?
A: Employees > select employee > Exit > Add Resignation. Enter resignation date, last working day and reason. Complete the exit checklist (asset return, clearance) and then run Full and Final settlement in Payroll.

Q: How do I search or filter employees?
A: On the Employees screen use the search bar for name, ID or mobile. Use Filter for department, branch, status or designation.

Q: How do I export the employee list?
A: Employees > Export (top right) > choose Excel or CSV. The file is emailed to you if it has more than 1,000 rows.
