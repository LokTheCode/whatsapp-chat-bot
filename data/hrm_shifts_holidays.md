Q: How do I see my shift timing?
A: Attendance > My Shift or Profile > Job Details shows your shift name, start time, end time and weekly off.

Q: How do I request a shift change?
A: Requests > Shift Change > select the date range and the new shift, add a reason and Submit. Your manager approves it.

Q: How do I swap a shift with a colleague?
A: Requests > Shift Swap > select the date and the colleague. Both the colleague and the manager must approve it.

Q: How does a manager publish a roster?
A: Menu > Roster > select the week or month, assign shifts to employees and tap Publish. Employees get a notification.

Q: Is there a night shift allowance?
A: If your company has a night shift allowance, it is added automatically to your payslip for shifts that cross the night-hours window. Check your salary structure for the rate.

Q: How is my weekly off decided?
A: Weekly off is set in your shift or roster. Fixed weekly offs repeat each week, rotational ones follow the roster.

Q: What is a rotational shift?
A: In rotational shifts the shift changes by week or month as set in the roster, for example Morning, General and Night in rotation.

Q: What are flexible working hours?
A: With flexible hours you must complete the total required hours for the day within a permitted window instead of fixed start and end times.

Q: Where can I see the holiday list?
A: Menu > Holidays shows the full year list for your branch, including national, festival and optional holidays.

Q: What are optional holidays and how do I use them?
A: Optional holidays are extra holidays from which you can pick a limited number. Go to Holidays > Optional > select the date and apply.

Q: How does admin add a holiday?
A: Admin: Settings > Holidays > Add Holiday, enter the name, date and branches it applies to and Save.

Q: Why is my branch holiday list different from a colleague's?
A: Holiday lists can differ by branch or state. The list shown is the one assigned to your branch.
