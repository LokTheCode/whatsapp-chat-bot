Q: How do I apply for leave?
A: Menu > Leave > Apply Leave. Choose the leave type, from and to dates, add a reason and tap Submit. Your manager receives the request for approval.

Q: What types of leave are available?
A: Common types are Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned/Privilege Leave, Comp-off, Maternity/Paternity and Loss of Pay. The types you see depend on your company's leave policy.

Q: How do I check my leave balance?
A: Menu > Leave > Balance. It shows each leave type with available, used and pending days.

Q: How do I cancel a leave request?
A: Leave > My Requests > select the request > Cancel. Pending leave can be cancelled directly. For already approved leave, the cancellation goes to your manager for approval.

Q: How long does leave approval take?
A: Your manager is notified immediately. If they do not respond within the time set by your company, the request is escalated or auto-approved depending on policy. You get a notification once it is decided.

Q: How do I apply for a half-day leave?
A: Apply Leave > select the date > turn on Half Day and choose First Half or Second Half. Half-day deducts 0.5 from your balance.

Q: What is comp-off and how do I apply?
A: Comp-off is leave given for working on a weekly off or holiday. Request it from Leave > Comp-off > Request, select the worked date and reason. After approval the credit appears in your balance.

Q: What is Loss of Pay (LOP)?
A: When your paid leave balance is zero, the extra days are treated as Loss of Pay and deducted from salary for those days.

Q: Does unused leave carry forward?
A: This depends on the leave type. Casual leave usually lapses at year end while earned leave may carry forward up to a limit. Check Leave > Policy for your rules.

Q: Do I need a document for sick leave?
A: If the sick leave is longer than the limit set by your company (often 2-3 days), attach a medical certificate in the Apply Leave form under Attachment.

Q: How do I apply for maternity or paternity leave?
A: Leave > Apply Leave > choose Maternity or Paternity Leave, select dates and attach supporting documents if requested. HR reviews and approves these requests.

Q: Are holidays and weekly offs counted as leave?
A: Not by default. If a holiday or weekly off falls inside your leave dates, it is not deducted, unless your policy says to count them (sandwich rule).

Q: Can I apply leave for past dates?
A: Backdated leave is allowed up to the number of days set by your company. Beyond that, ask HR to apply it for you.

Q: How can I see the team leave calendar?
A: Leave > Team Calendar shows who is on leave on each date, so you can plan your own leave.

Q: Where can I read my company leave policy?
A: Leave > Policy shows the leave types, yearly quota, carry-forward and rules applicable to you.

Q: How does a manager approve or reject leave?
A: Menu > Approvals > Leave. Open the request and tap Approve or Reject, adding a comment if needed. The employee is notified.
